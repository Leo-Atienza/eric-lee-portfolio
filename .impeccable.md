## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and analysts at finance, accounting and consulting employers in the
GTA, opening the link from a résumé or LinkedIn on a laptop during the workday. Job to be done:
decide in under two minutes whether Eric can be trusted with their numbers, then find the résumé
and an email address. Secondary: an interviewer opening a dashboard capture during a call.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **ledgered, unhurried, exact.** The physical object the site imitates is a bound
financial statement on ruled ledger paper: hairline rules that carry rows, figures set in tabular
numerals, one quiet accent, nothing decorative that does not hold data. Emotional goal: the
reader feels the same calm they feel opening a well-prepared set of accounts.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Genre:** editorial · **Register:** brand · **Dials:** ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2
- **Theme:** light by default (daytime, office, printed-report register); dark retained as a
  deliberate second costume (ledger ink inverted) because the OS preference toggle already
  exists and `enableSystem` honours it. Derived, unconfirmed.
- **Palette family:** `ledger-green`, one hue, varied lightness. Ground is barely-green ruled
  paper (chroma ≈ 0.006, felt not seen), ink is deep bottle green-black, the single accent is a
  deeper ledger green on ≤ 3 surfaces (primary link, current nav shortcut, the rule under h1).
  Chosen instead of the warm-paper + oxblood triad (AR-70 premium-consumer default) and instead
  of navy (the reflex finance colour). The green comes from the subject's own world: accounting
  ledger paper.
- **Type:** display **Libre Caslon Display** (Caslon set the 18th-century financial and legal
  page; Benjamin Franklin printed in it) + body **Public Sans** (Libre Franklin lineage:
  Franklin the printer, then Franklin Gothic; institutional, exact, tabular figures). Two
  families, two files, self-hosted, no mono.
- **References studied (reference-sites.md):** Klim ("specimen as essay", real running copy,
  a live size ramp), Rauno Freiberg (near-monochrome + hairlines + systemised spacing), Hermès
  (colour and motion live in the chrome, never on the goods).
- **Anti-references:** the previous costume (dark canvas, blue→violet gradient text, glass cards,
  glow, floating pills, tilt/magnetic hover, proficiency bars with invented percentages);
  fintech dashboards (Revolut/Coinbase dark gradients); the saturated editorial lane (italic
  serif display + tiny mono labels + ruled three-column separators).

## Costume & Theme (from the design consultation)

- Look source: **redesign, keep the soul (content, sections, order), change the costume**
- Costume: **house-made "ledger", invented from the subject's world**, not a library pick;
  nearest library neighbours are cartesian (hairline rigor) and editorial-forest (green), both
  rejected as-is (cartesian has zero colour, editorial-forest carries pink + mono)
- Theme: light default, dark second (derived, see above)
- Nav N1b (wordmark left, section shortcuts in the middle, résumé and theme right; under 64rem
  the shortcuts open a sheet). Hero H2 split diptych 7/5: name, one line and two actions left,
  the portrait plate right. Section heads S2, no ledes except Contact. Skills F3 spec sheet.
  Experience F4 dated sequence. Figures T4 sourced stat strip. CTAs C3. Footer Ft2.
- Copy rule (Eric, 2026-09-08): fewest words that carry the fact. Ghost rules apply: no dashes,
  plain words, active voice.

## Design Principles

1. **Every rule carries a row.** Hairlines exist to separate data (rows, columns, entries), never
   as decoration between sections. If a line holds nothing, delete it.
2. **Figures are set like money.** Tabular numerals, right-aligned in columns, every number
   sourced to the résumé or a project document and footnoted. No metric Eric did not supply.
3. **One accent, rationed.** The ledger green appears on at most three surfaces per viewport.
   Emphasis otherwise comes from size, weight and space.
4. **Motion at M2, and only where a reader would look.** One entrance choreography on the hero,
   reveal-once on sections, counting figures. No parallax, no cursor-following, no bounce.
5. **Real material only.** The portrait, the dashboard captures and the résumé are the imagery.
   No icons in tiles, no stock, no generated art.
